# Verified references / 已核实参考资料

Initial records checked 2026-09-14; Dendukuri & Joseph equation-image audit recorded 2026-09-21; Toft access record checked 2026-09-20. DOI links are source-verified, not inferred. Full-text access limits are recorded. Only statistics and diagnostic-evaluation guidance are used.

1. WOAH. Terrestrial Manual, Chapter 1.1.6, Validation of diagnostic assays for infectious diseases of terrestrial animals. [Official PDF, 2023 label](https://www.woah.org/fileadmin/Home/eng/Health_standards/tahm/1.01.06_VALIDATION.pdf). Current SONT chapter RID 268 retrieved via official WOAH API. Purpose: study metadata, source/target populations, imperfect standards, LCM assumptions. No DOI.
2. WOAH. Chapter 2.2.3, Development and optimisation of nucleic acid detection assays. Current SONT RID 282, text states updates adopted 2025. [Older static PDF](https://www.woah.org/fileadmin/Home/eng/Health_standards/tahm/2.02.03_NAD_ASSAYS.pdf). Purpose: intended diagnostic use and distinction of analytical from diagnostic performance. No laboratory instructions incorporated.
3. WOAH. Chapter 2.2.5, Statistical approaches to validation. Current SONT RID 284, updates adopted 2025. [Official manual portal](https://sont.woah.org/portal/tool?le=en). Purpose: design, latent class estimation, sample-size limitations, STARD-BLCM reporting.
4. WOAH. Chapter 2.2.6, Selection and use of reference samples and panels. Current SONT RID 285. Purpose: reference-sample applicability. [Official standards](https://www.woah.org/en/what-we-do/standards/codes-and-manuals/).
5. Dendukuri N, Joseph L. Bayesian Approaches to Modeling the Conditional Dependence Between Multiple Diagnostic Tests. Biometrics. 2001;57(1):158–167. DOI [10.1111/j.0006-341X.2001.00158.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.0006-341X.2001.00158.x). PMID [11252592](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11252592/). [Author-hosted full text](https://joseph.research.mcgill.ca/publications/Methodological/conddep_diag.pdf), locally archived with SHA-256; selected equation images checked, recorded 2026-09-21. See SOURCE_AUDIT_20260921.md for the distinction between the printed posterior kernel and VetBLCM's explicitly normalized conditional covariance prior; no exact Gibbs reproduction is claimed.
6. Branscum AJ, Gardner IA, Johnson WO. Estimation of diagnostic-test sensitivity and specificity through Bayesian modeling. Preventive Veterinary Medicine. 2005;68(2–4):145–163. DOI [10.1016/j.prevetmed.2004.12.005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.prevetmed.2004.12.005). Publisher record plus full published-paper PDF read. Purpose: sections 3.2 and 3.3.3 likelihoods, covariance priors, multiple-population non-identifiability and prior sensitivity. No source code copied.
7. Gardner IA, Stryhn H, Lind P, Collins MT. Conditional dependence between tests affects the diagnosis and surveillance of animal diseases. Preventive Veterinary Medicine. 2000;45(1–2):107–122. DOI [10.1016/S0167-5877(00)00119-7](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0167-5877(00)00119-7). PMID [10802336](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10802336/). Abstract verified; supports dependence implications, not universal positive dependence for every PCR pair.
8. Johnson WO, Gastwirth JL, Pearson LM. Screening without a "gold standard": the Hui-Walter paradigm revisited. American Journal of Epidemiology. 2001;153(9):921–924. DOI [10.1093/aje/153.9.921](https://doi.org/10.1093/aje/153.9.921). PMID [11323324](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11323324/). Abstract verified. Purpose: identifiable independent design versus non-identifiable single population.
9. Toft N, Jørgensen E, Højsgaard S. Diagnosing diagnostic tests: evaluating the assumptions underlying the estimation of sensitivity and specificity in the absence of a gold standard. Preventive Veterinary Medicine. 2005;68(1):19–33. DOI [10.1016/j.prevetmed.2005.01.006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.prevetmed.2005.01.006). University and publisher metadata/abstract verified; [DTU record](https://orbit.dtu.dk/en/publications/diagnosing-diagnostic-tests-evaluating-the-assumptions-underlying/) rechecked 2026-09-20. Full-text review PENDING_VERIFICATION. Purpose: assumption assessment; no unverified equation implemented from this paper.
10. Hui SL, Walter SD. Estimating the error rates of diagnostic tests. Biometrics. 1980;36(1):167–171. PMID [7370371](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7370371/). DOI not entered because not independently confirmed. Purpose: classical two-test, two-population model; computational likelihood also verified in reference 6.
11. Erdely A. A Subcopula Characterization of Dependence for the Multivariate Bernoulli Distribution. Journal of Statistical Theory and Applications. 2026;25:4. DOI [10.1007/s44199-025-00157-4](https://doi.org/10.1007/s44199-025-00157-4). Full-text equations 7–8 verified. Purpose: complete bivariate Bernoulli feasibility bounds. Mathematical identities independently implemented; no article code, figures or adapted prose redistributed.
12. Plummer M. JAGS Version 4.3.0 User Manual. [Official project](https://mcmc-jags.sourceforge.io/); [manual distribution](https://sourceforge.net/projects/mcmc-jags/files/Manuals/4.x/). Official manual text retrieved from university-hosted copy. Purpose: BUGS syntax, dbeta, dmulti, truncation. Tested engine: JAGS 4.3.1.
13. Plummer M. rjags: Bayesian Graphical Models using MCMC. [CRAN manual](https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/rjags/rjags.pdf). Current upstream 4-17; installed 4-16. Purpose: adaptation, sampling and reproducible initialization; local help used to verify installed API.
14. Vehtari A, Gelman A, Simpson D, Carpenter B, Bürkner PC. Rank-normalization, folding, and localization: An improved R-hat for assessing convergence of MCMC (with discussion). Bayesian Analysis. 2021;16(2):667–718. DOI [10.1214/20-BA1221](https://doi.org/10.1214/20-BA1221). [posterior R-hat](https://mc-stan.org/posterior/reference/rhat.html), [bulk ESS](https://mc-stan.org/posterior/reference/ess_bulk.html), [tail ESS](https://mc-stan.org/posterior/reference/ess_tail.html), [current diagnostic guidance](https://mc-stan.org/docs/reference-manual/analysis.html).
15. Stan Development Team. [Posterior and Prior Predictive Checks](https://mc-stan.org/docs/stan-users-guide/posterior-predictive-checks.html). Purpose: simulation-based fit assessment. This conceptual reference does not make Stan a required backend.
16. FDA. Statistical Guidance on Reporting Results from Studies Evaluating Diagnostic Tests. March 2007. [Official guidance](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/statistical-guidance-reporting-results-studies-evaluating-diagnostic-tests-guidance-industry-and-fda). 17. Kostoulas P, Nielsen SS, Branscum AJ, Johnson WO, Dendukuri N, Dhand NK, Toft N, Gardner IA. STARD-BLCM: Standards for the Reporting of Diagnostic accuracy studies that use Bayesian Latent Class Models. Preventive Veterinary Medicine. 2017;138:37–47. DOI [10.1016/j.prevetmed.2017.01.006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.prevetmed.2017.01.006). PMID [28237234](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28237234/). Purpose: reporting topics for diagnostic-accuracy studies using Bayesian latent class models.
